Jamdung - Jamaica Quiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Jamdung - Jamaica Quiz is a clue quiz game about Jamaica made by Realisticworks, for players 13 and older. The game has no accounts, and your progress is saved on your device. The free game shows ads from Google AdMob, and you can buy gems or Remove ads through your app store. Those services collect some information, as explained below. If you leave "Share anonymous play stats" on, the game also sends us anonymous play stats, so we know when to add levels and which ones are too hard. We never collect your name, email, contacts or location.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in and no chat with other players.
  • Your levels, stars, gems and settings are saved on your device.
  • Optional anonymous play stats: how far you've got and how you play, under a random player code (no name, no advertising ID). You can turn them off in Settings.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob, which collects device and usage information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases go through Google Play or the Amazon Appstore. We never see your card or payment details.
  • The daily reminder and sharing with friends work on your device; nothing about them is sent to us.

Play stats (optional)

To know when to add new levels, and which levels are too hard, the game can send us anonymous play stats. You can turn this off at any time in Settings ("Share anonymous play stats"). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, it stays off until you turn it on.

The stats are sent now and then (about once an hour at most, while you play) and contain:

  • a random player code that the game makes on your device (it isn't your advertising ID or any other device ID, and it isn't linked to your other apps or accounts);
  • the app version, the store you got the game from (Google Play or Amazon), your device's language and whether it's a phone or a tablet;
  • your progress: levels solved (overall, in each topic and in the last 30 days), stars, streaks, trophies and gems;
  • how you play: which of the last 90 days you played, how many times and for how many minutes, the hours of the day you open levels, levels solved on each of the last 30 days, Blitz runs and your best Blitz score, Bonus rounds, which hints you used, videos and ads watched, chests opened, how often you shared (and to which app), how often you played on a TV, the last level you opened and the levels you left unsolved most often;
  • how your last 120 solved levels went: the level's code, stars, hints, wrong guesses, seconds taken, and whether you used the big clue or asked a friend (never what you typed);
  • your game settings: whether sound, vibration, Easy letters, Calm mode and the daily reminder are on or off.

The stats never include your name, email, contacts, location, advertising ID, payment details or anything you type. They're kept in a Google Sheet that only we can open, which Google hosts for us (Google Apps Script); like any web server, Google's servers see the network address a request comes from. We use the stats only to improve the game, never for advertising, and we don't sell them or share them with anyone. We keep each player's latest stats (one record per player code, replaced each time new stats arrive) while the game is offered, and delete them sooner if you ask.

To have your stats deleted, email us your player code, which the game shows under Settings > About. Turning off "Share anonymous play stats" stops any more stats from being sent.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Now and then the game shows a full-screen ad between levels. You can also choose to watch a short video, for the big clue or for a letter when you're out of gems. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app collects:

  • your device's network (IP) address, which gives a rough idea of where you are (a city or region, not your exact location);
  • how you use the ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • information about how the app and the ad software are working (diagnostics);
  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID.

This information goes to Google. Google explains how it uses it in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • Where the law requires it (such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form before any ads load, and ads use your information only as you choose there. You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings, under "Ad privacy choices".
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off personalized ads, in your device's settings (on most Android devices: Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for teens.
  • Buying Remove ads turns off the ads between levels, and the big clue and the out-of-gems letters come without a video.

Gems and Remove ads (in-app purchases)

You can buy gem packs, a one-time starter pack and Remove ads. You pay Google Play or the Amazon Appstore, under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your card or payment details. The store tells the game what you bought so it can add your gems, and the game keeps that record only on your device. Remove ads belongs to your store account, so "Restore purchases" in Settings brings it back on a new device or after reinstalling. Gems are kept on your device and can't be brought back that way.

Rating the game

Now and then the game may show your store's own rating card (Google Play's in-app review). Your rating and review go to the store. The game never learns whether you rated it, or what you said.

Daily reminder

If you turn on "Daily reminder" in Settings, the game asks your device to show one notification a day, only on days you haven't played yet, around the time you usually play (Android 13 and newer first ask whether the game may show notifications). The reminder is set up and shown on your device, and nothing about it is sent anywhere. Turn it off in Settings, or in your device's notification settings.

Sharing with friends

"Ask for help" and "Challenge a friend" make a picture of a level's clues or of your Blitz score, with a short message and a link to the game, and hand them to the app you choose (such as WhatsApp, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or Messages). That app sends or posts them, under its own privacy policy. The game doesn't see who you share with, and nothing is sent to us. The picture is kept in the game's own temporary storage until you share again.

To offer WhatsApp, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook by name, the game checks whether those apps are on your device. It doesn't look for any other apps, and the answer stays on your device. When you post to TikTok, Instagram or Facebook, which don't accept a ready-written message, a short caption is copied to your device's clipboard for you to paste.

Playing on your TV

With a cable or screen mirroring (such as Smart View), the picture goes straight from your device to the TV, and nothing is sent over the internet. With a Chromecast or Google TV (in the Google Play version), Google Cast connects your device to the TV. The TV then loads the game's TV screen from our website and Google's Cast software from Google. Like any website, those servers see the TV's network address. Google's Cast software also sends Google anonymous logs about the connection, with nothing in them that identifies you.

New levels

The whole game is inside the app, and you can play it offline. It goes online to check for new quiz levels and download them. They come as a small file of questions kept on GitHub (gist.githubusercontent.com). Nothing about you or your device is sent with that request. Like any website, GitHub's servers see the network address a request comes from, and GitHub's own privacy statement covers that. If you're offline, the game keeps the levels it has. The ads and videos need the internet; when you're offline, the big clue costs gems instead of a video.

Your progress

Your levels, stars, gems and settings are saved on your own device, inside the app. If play stats are on, a summary of your progress and your on/off game settings are part of them (see "Play stats" above); what you type is never sent. Uninstalling the app, or choosing "Reset progress" in Settings, deletes your progress from the device, including gems you bought.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download new levels, show ads, make purchases, send play stats (if they're on) and connect to a TV.
  • Vibration: a short buzz when you tap or win. You can switch it off in Settings.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and newer ask first): only for the daily reminder, if you turn it on.
  • Run at startup: so a daily reminder you've turned on still comes after your device restarts.
  • Advertising ID and Android's ad services: used by Google AdMob, as described above.
  • Billing (Google Play version): to buy gems and Remove ads through Google Play.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: added by Google's ad and Cast software to finish short background tasks.

The game doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Jamdung - Jamaica Quiz is meant for players 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.
